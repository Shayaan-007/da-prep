# Firm processes, wave 2 (research, 2 October 2026)

Firms: Aviva, Cisco, Google, J.P. Morgan, IBM, HSBC, BMW Group, Experian, Microsoft, Santander.

Method as in `03-firm-processes-wave1.md`. **This wave was mostly negative on first-hand evidence.** Almost every employer careers page for these firms was blocked (403), redirected or returned only navigation text. Glassdoor, TheStudentRoom, Reddit, Gradcracker and Bright Network returned 403 to plain fetches. Anything seen only through a search-result summary is labelled "search summary only" in the profiles and was not read as a page.

Result: **no new reported interview questions were added** to any of these ten profiles, because no question could be tied to a page that was actually read. The research mainly corrected overstated or unsupported claims and recorded what is and is not confirmed.

## What changed, by firm

- **HSBC:** removed a claim that HSBC cancelled some 2025 starts (it appeared only in a search summary of forum threads, with no official or news support). Added HSBC's official apprentice facts (about 3 years, no tuition fees, 16 or older, UK resident, not in full-time education, locations). Recorded reported close dates (6 November 2024 and 2025) and a possible 28 September to 1 November 2026 window (unconfirmed). Flagged a possible degree-provider discrepancy (Exeter versus LIBF).
- **BMW Group:** a search extract of BMW's own page lists three steps (application, video interview, assessment centre) with no online assessment. The online assessment stage is now marked unconfirmed for apprentices; the Criteria RCAT claim comes from a generic BMW Group source.
- **IBM:** 2026 Level 6 adverts say "No online assessment required", so the online assessment is conditional. Deadlines conflict by pathway (4 March, 27 May 2026; 17 August 2025). Added salary (£26k 2025, £27k+ 2026) and the security-clearance requirement.
- **Aviva:** the gov.uk listing shows the 2026 Software Engineer vacancy closed on 16 March 2026, so Aviva looks like a September start with a mid-March close rather than "rolling". Video timings softened (graduate-route reports only).
- **Cisco:** only the programmes page was readable. Added the reported assessment-centre activities (single report), the BA/BSc discrepancy for Professional Management, and flagged the Velocity, HireVue and 104-point claims as unconfirmed.
- **Google:** no evidence of a UK Level 6 degree apprenticeship. Added the Level 3 programmes reported in search summaries. Older read pages (2021, 2023) are historical.
- **J.P. Morgan:** official page confirms entry grades and locations but no stages or dates. A Technology programme deadline of about 2 November 2025 is a single unread report. Outdated WikiJob grades are not used.
- **Experian:** Experian's page confirms the four stages and names no test vendor; SHL is now a prep-site claim only.
- **Microsoft:** added a Cheltenham Level 6 Cyber Security listing (single third-party source) and its entry requirements.
- **Santander:** added salary (£27,500), hybrid pattern, and that the games and video interview are one combined step for selected roles.

## Browser pass (2 Oct 2026, same day)

A real browser (Chrome automation, public logged-out pages only, essential cookies only, read-only) reads sources that plain fetches cannot:
- **Works:** careers.aviva.co.uk (loads; its text needs the page body, not just `<main>`), bmwgroup.jobs, jpmorganchase.com, and **TheStudentRoom threads** (no CAPTCHA).
- **Blocked by a bot check, not attempted further:** **Glassdoor** ("Humans only" / "Just a moment"). It was not bypassed and should not be, so Glassdoor remains unavailable as a source.
- **Genuine 404s (pages removed, not blocked):** BMW's UK recruitment-process page and the whole `/gb/en/apprentices` section; J.P. Morgan's technology apprenticeship page.
- **Yield was low.** Aviva's official process page is generic. TheStudentRoom threads confirm existing claims (HSBC Codility timings, no assessment centre, three final interviews; J.P. Morgan assessment evening with a group task) but candidates rarely post real questions there, so **no new reported questions were added**.
- The browser extension disconnected partway through, so HSBC's own degree-apprenticeship page, Cisco and Google were not opened.
