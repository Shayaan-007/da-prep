# Assessment formats (research, 2 October 2026)

Purpose: the facts we build format-faithful replicas from. Replicas use ORIGINAL items; we never copy vendors' questions.

Confidence tags: **[O]** official source, **[M]** multiple prep/candidate reports, **[S]** single report, **[U]** unverified.

Where an official page could not be fetched (HTTP 403 or unreadable PDF) the fact comes from a search-result summary and is tagged accordingly. Many vendors do not publish calculator rules, go-back rules, norm groups or cut-offs: those are **[U]** everywhere below and the replicas label them "approximate".

## Cut-e / Aon (scales)

- Scales Numerical: 37 items, 12 min; short form 18 items, 6 min. True/false/cannot say, data across multiple tabs (tables, graphs, charts). **[M]**
- Scales Verbal: 49 items, 12 min, true/false/cannot say. **[M]**
- Logical: ~12 min per test, several inductive/deductive variants. **[M]**
- No official Aon candidate guide found. Calculator, go-back, norms, cut-offs **[U]**.

## Talent Q / Korn Ferry

- Elements: ~15 min per test; verbal ~15 items, numerical ~12, logical ~12; adaptive. **[M]**
- Dimensions: no format found. **[U]**

## Criteria CCAT

- 50 questions, 15 min, one section, mix of spatial, verbal and maths/logic. **[O]**
- Standardisation sample mean 24.2, SD 8.58. **[O]**. Most people do not finish, by design.
- Calculator: not on the official page; prep sites say none. **[M]**

## Arctic Shores, Pymetrics / Harver (no right answers)

- Arctic Shores: task-based, gamified; reported to measure ~13 traits (risk appetite, learning agility, persistence, resilience, processing speed, attention control...). **[S]**, official help pages blocked. Task count and duration **[U]**.
- Pymetrics/Harver: 12 short games, ~25 min; trait profile compared to a benchmark. **[M]** Game names reported: Balloons, Towers, Money Exchange 1 and 2, Keypresses, Digits, Cards, Arrows, Lengths, Easy or Hard, Stop, Facial Expressions.

## Civil Service (all **[O]**)

- Civil Service Judgement Test: Part 1 self-assessment (15%), Part 2 SJT (85%); 3 scenarios per behaviour, each with 4 actions rated Counterproductive / Ineffective / Fairly Effective / Effective; untimed (allow about an hour); video or text scenarios; percentile scoring with a minimum standard per level. Go-back **[U]**.
- Verbal and numerical reasoning: untimed, adaptive, no skipping or going back; most people take 15-45 min each; calculator and rough paper allowed; true/false/cannot say verbally; graphs and tables numerically; item count not stated **[U]**; scores banked six months within a grade grouping.

## Capp / Cappfinity

- Strengths-based SJT: rate likelihood of choosing each response or how much you would enjoy it. **[S]**. Item count and timing **[U]**.

## HireVue

- Typical: 30 s prep, up to 3 min answer per question; most assessments under 20-30 min; practice question available; retakes set by employer (none or one re-record common). **[M]**
- Number of questions set by employer, usually 3-6 **[U]**.
- HireVue states it stopped visual analysis in 2021; where AI scoring is used it scores the transcript against an employer-configured rubric. **[M]**
