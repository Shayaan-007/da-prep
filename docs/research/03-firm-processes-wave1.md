# Firm processes, wave 1 (research, 2 October 2026)

Firms: PwC, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, Barclays, Rolls-Royce, BAE Systems, Lloyds Banking Group.

Method: official firm pages first, then candidate-experience sources. Confidence tags as in `02-assessment-formats.md`. Glassdoor, TheStudentRoom and Reddit could not be read directly (403 / blocked), so candidate claims come from search-result summaries and are at most **[S]** or **[M]**. Many prep sites describe the graduate route: not used as apprentice fact.

## PwC (lowest confidence)
- pwc.co.uk returned 403; every claim is from search excerpts of PwC text or prep sites.
- Most Flying Start programmes are applied for through UCAS with a course code; if the university accepts you, you go through PwC's process. One application per cycle.
- Stage list conflicts. PwC's own page (excerpt) lists five stages: application form; immersive job preview (SJT, ~15 short-video questions); games-based psychometric assessment; SHL video interview; virtual immersive assessment centre. Other sources give different stage names and a cognitive test.
- Video format conflicts: 6 questions with 30 s prep and 2 min answer; 10 questions with 2 min prep and 3 min answer; 3-6 questions with no retakes. Assessment centre reports range from ~1h40 with up to 5 candidates to a full day.
- Reported questions (single report): why Flying Start and why you fit; what would you do when you face a problem; your motivations for the role.

## Deloitte (official pages read)
- Five steps: application (~15 min); immersive online assessment (behavioural: rank most/least likely actions; plus numerical and verbal; some sections timed, no overall limit; decision within 4 weeks); job simulation (ranking, selection, written and video responses, includes AI scenarios; results within 4 weeks); final stage (30-40 min topic discussion plus 50 min skills and motivation interview, preparation required; in person for graduates, entry-level apprentices and BrightStart); outcome with learning resources.
- Entry-level apprenticeships do application plus OneStop (assessment and simulation combined). One application per academic year; roles close early under high volume.
- BrightStart entry: 104 UCAS points from top three A-levels, not already graduated; many business areas, UK offices. Entry-level: Cardiff Delivery Centre, 80/20 work/study.
- Timings of the OA/simulation (~30/~40 min) are TargetJobs only **[S]**; Deloitte publishes none.
- Reported BrightStart questions **[S]**: why Deloitte; what do you know about Deloitte; a time you worked successfully in a team; a time you came up with a creative idea; a setback on a project. Glassdoor BrightStart summary also mentions ~3 days to prepare a presentation topic and a group assessment; conflicts with the official format, so treat as office-dependent.

## KPMG (official page read)
- Application: job preview quiz plus form (~45 min). Assessments: untimed skills assessment (~20 min) and a timed 36-min cognitive test. Video: 6 pre-recorded questions from an AI avatar (~40 min allowed). Launch Pad: in-person half day; offer within 2 working days.
- 2026/27 Launch Pad dates **[O]**: 17 Nov 2026 London; 1 Dec Birmingham; 9 Dec Manchester; 16 Mar 2027 Bristol; 23 Mar Glasgow; 27 Apr London; 5 May Leeds.
- Programmes: Audit; Tax & Law; Technology & Engineering; Consulting; Corporate Services (2-year Business Administration); 19 offices.
- Launch Pad contents **[S]**: group exercise (~1 h), analysis task, one-to-one senior interview. Reported senior-interview questions **[S]**: why KPMG and this service line; most challenging task; a time you diffused team tension; a technology with big impact in the last five years.

## EY (official pages read)
- Stages: search and apply; assessment prep hub; online assessment (Realistic Job Preview for teamwork and learning capability, plus behavioural skills testing; may include multiple choice, chart interpretation, pattern identification, video responses; timed); Experience Day; onboarding.
- Apprentices attend the Experience Day **in person** at an EY office (virtual only in exceptional circumstances). Apprentices wait 3 months after a rejection. Entry: GCSE English and Maths 4/C plus three A-levels or equivalent. Roles close when filled.
- Experience Day agenda (case prep 60 min + 15 min presentation + 15 min Q&A; group exercise 40-50 min; competency interview 45 min; partner interview) is **[S]**, graduate-oriented.
- Reported 2025 questions **[S]**: why Tax; what strengths would help; how you balance work and learning; what research you did for the role; why this location.

## Barclays (official page read)
- Five steps: role selection; application form (~30 min); initial assessment (two interactive assessments, ~60 min total, to complete within 5 calendar days, any device); interview with leadership plus group activity; offer or feedback.
- Using third-party AI tools terminates the interview and withdraws the application **[O]**. Reapply after 6 months.
- Graduate-route details (HireVue 5-7 questions, 60-90 min tests) must not be applied to apprentices: the apprentice journey lists no video stage.
- Entry requirements conflict between the apprenticeship-programmes page (Level 3/4: 5 GCSEs at 9-5 incl. Maths and English; £25,200) and degree/higher routes. Test vendor, item counts, whether the group activity runs in practice: **[U]**.

## Rolls-Royce (2026 official guide read from local PDF)
- Degree and higher apprentices attend an **in-person** half-day assessment centre: interview (7 questions for degree apprentices), a prepared presentation (7 min for engineering, short Q&A; no overhead projector in person), and a technical exercise responding to a case study given on the day. Level 3: practical activity and a 5 min presentation.
- Behaviours rated 1-6 with positive and negative indicators. Behaviours: Put safety first; Do the right thing; Keep it simple; Make a difference. The trend question may repeat something from the application form.
- Entry (search excerpt of official page): 5 GCSEs 4/C incl. English Language, Maths and Science; 112 UCAS points from three A-levels at BBC incl. Maths; alternatives BTEC/T Level; starting salary £21,776. 2027 degree apprenticeships: register interest; many UK sites.

## BAE Systems (current official page read)
- Closed for 2026; reopens January 2027, main window ~6 weeks from early January, further hiring through February. Process: application; virtual assessment with gamified challenges and video questions; virtual or face-to-face interview. Entry: age 16+, 5 GCSEs 4-9 incl. Maths and English, minimum 96 points (England/Wales); vetting (BPSS minimum; residency rules may apply); salary up to £26,000. Old URLs redirect (301).
- Reported 2026 format **[S]**: HireVue-style recording 3-5 past-experience questions (retakes reported), then three games (shape matching, a maths game, image-choice "which are you more like"). Video prep time conflicts (30 s vs 3 min).
- Reported questions **[S]**: a time you had little time to make a choice; why BAE as an apprentice; a time you met requirements outside your comfort zone.

## Lloyds Banking Group (official pages read)
- Three stages: application; online assessments (motivation, behaviour, values, written communication; have pen, paper and calculator); interview or assessment day (varies by apprenticeship; Events Platform or in person).
- Opportunities open 3 November (year inferred: 2026). Locations: Birmingham, Bristol, Chester, Edinburgh, Halifax, Leeds, London, Manchester.
- Assessment-centre contents **[S]** and conflicting: 1-hour strengths-based interview plus four 10-minute micro-exercises, group exercise and a 15-minute VR exercise (higher apprenticeships) vs shorter exercises plus numerical and group exercise (degree apprentices) vs a virtual case study (graduates).
- Reported questions **[S]**, strengths-based: what friends and family know you for; what you enjoy and are like at your best; achievements and how; what a great day looks like; what you do not enjoy; how you will put the customer first. The Lloyds of London site is a different company.
